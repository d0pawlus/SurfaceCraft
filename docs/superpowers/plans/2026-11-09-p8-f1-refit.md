# P8-F1: CAD Correct / Refit (design note)

Goal: update an existing CAD body so that it matches a new scan of the changed part (as-built). The scan is
already aligned to the body. Designed from this description and public literature (robust and
Levenberg-Marquardt least squares, nearest-surface association, offset surfaces); nothing from a closed product.

## `body.refit_features` (body with a feature history)
- **Parameters**: sketch dimensions (`length`, `distance`, `radius`; `angle` on request), extrude depth, fillet and
  chamfer size, shell thickness (draft angles on request). Ids `sketch/<id>/<constraint>`, `feature/<id>/<field>`;
  `params` lists ids or whole objects, default is every dimension-like one of the features that make the body
  (and the bodies and sketches they read). Bounds: +-`max_change` (angles +-`max_angle_change`), sign kept.
- **Objective**: signed distance of the scan points to the tessellated body (`deviation::against_mesh`), Huber
  robustified (`fit::lm::robust_residual`), points beyond `max_distance` ignored. Each evaluation writes the trial
  values into a scratch document, re-solves the touched sketches and replays (`replay_doc`), tessellates with a
  fixed chord, builds a BVH.
- **Solver**: `fit::lm::minimize_bounded`, Levenberg-Marquardt with box bounds (active set), absolute
  finite-difference step (the replay is piecewise smooth), failed replays are rejected trials. Afterwards each
  changed parameter is put back if reverting it costs less than 2e-4 mm RMS (a cut deeper than the part moves
  only on difference noise). Reports old/new, bounds, sensitivity, RMS before/after; applied as one undoable step.

## `body.refit_faces` (body without history)
- **Faces**: `kernel::refit::face_shapes` reads each record face as an oriented analytic shape (plane, cylinder,
  cone, sphere, torus; a zero-angle cone is a cylinder) with signed distance and outward normal. Spline, ball patch
  and swept-profile faces are refused with a reason and keep their place.
- **Association**: per face a BVH of its triangles; a scan point goes to the nearest face (within `max_distance`)
  whose outward normal is within `max_angle` of the point's normal.
- **Motion**: the kernel offers one move per face, `ops::offset_faces` along the outward normal (plane moves,
  cylinder/sphere/torus change radius, cone slides). The signed distance field of a parallel surface shifts by the
  offset, so the fit is a 1-D regularised robust mean per group: `theta = sum w d / (sum w + sum omega n)`.
  Stiffness is the weight omega of the original against the face's own scan evidence: `fixed` infinity, `tight` 2,
  `loose` 0.05, `free` 0. Moves are clamped to +-`max_change` and skipped below `min_move`.
- **Relations**: faces on one surface move together; with `keep_relations`, cylinders, spheres and tube radii of
  equal radius share one radius change (sign by convex/concave); a fixed member holds its group. Orientation
  relations (parallel, perpendicular, coaxial) are kept by construction (an offset changes neither normal nor
  axis). Counts of all four are reported before and after the kernel rebuild.
- **Rebuild**: one kernel call per surface (`kernel::refit::offset_face_groups`), curved faces first; a face is
  found again in the running body by its surface. A refused move is reported per face; the rest is applied.
  Per face old/new parameters (read back from the rebuilt body) and RMS before/after (same points).

## Limits
No tilt or axis-shift of a face (the kernel has no such move besides draft); tangent blends follow a moved
neighbour, so a second look at the report is advised; bodies of several shells and more than 3000 faces are
refused; the feature refit needs the history to replay with the trial values (a failing trial is just rejected).
